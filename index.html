          Accessibility

          Text size

          Larger text, stronger contrast on labels and small print, thick focus outlines, calm motion (no shaking or flashing, shorter animations), and states shown with a mark or a word, never by colour alone.

          Language / Idioma

          Audio

          Presentation pace

          The table as designed: each rule plays out step by step, one thing at a time.

          Comic effects

          Big moments get the full comic panel; routine rules get a small stamp or inked word.
